Privacy

Your Privacy Choices

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Kinor LLC, which does business as Kinor Partners, does not sell personal information. This website does not share personal information for targeted advertising, and it does not load advertising pixels. What we do collect is described in our privacy policy.

Do not sell or share

You can still send a do-not-sell or do-not-share request. Email info@kinorpartners.com with the subject "Privacy request", the state you live in, and the email address you want us to match.

Global Privacy Control

Global Privacy Control (GPC) is a browser signal that asks a site not to sell or share personal information. Because we do not sell or share personal information, there is no sale or share for that signal to turn off. If your browser has GPC on and you want the request on file, say so in the same email.

Other privacy requests

The same email address is how you ask to know, correct, or delete personal information, or how you appeal a denial. The US state privacy rights section of the privacy policy lists those rights and what to include.

Send a privacy request

Email info@kinorpartners.com. Kinor LLC, Los Angeles, California, United States.
