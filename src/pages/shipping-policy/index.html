Policy

Shipping Policy

Last updated: June 4, 2026

Where we ship

We offer free shipping within the United States on physical products. For delivery outside the United States, email info@kinorpartners.com before ordering and we will confirm availability and any applicable cost.

Processing and delivery

Orders are typically processed within 1-2 business days. Once shipped, U.S. delivery usually takes 3-7 business days depending on your location. You will receive tracking information by email when your order ships.

Returns

Need to send something back? See our return policy for the 30-day return window, return shipping costs, and how to start a return.

Services and digital plans

Service and subscription plans are delivered electronically and do not require shipping. Access and onboarding details are provided by email after purchase.

Questions about your order?

Email us at info@kinorpartners.com or visit our Contact page.
