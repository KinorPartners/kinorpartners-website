Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This privacy policy describes how Kinor LLC, which does business as Kinor Partners ("Kinor," "we," "us"), collects, uses, and shares personal information on kinorpartners.com (the "site"). Kinor LLC is based in Los Angeles, California, United States.

California notice: if you live in California, the US state privacy rights section below is our notice at collection. It explains the information we collect and the rights you can use.

On this page: Information we collect - Cookies and analytics - How we use it - How we share it - How long we keep it - Your choices - US state privacy rights - Children - Security - Contact

Personal information we collect

We collect only what this site actually asks for or what its tools record. We do not run a customer account system on this site, and we do not collect payment card numbers here. Product checkout for the Shirt Tucker happens on shirt-tucker.com, which has its own site and its own privacy practices.

Information you give us

  • Contact forms. The form on the Contact page asks for your name, email address, company or website (optional), and message. The free-audit form on the home page asks for your name, email address, and message. Both forms are sent to FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which emails the contents to info@kinorpartners.com.
  • Email. If you email info@kinorpartners.com, we keep the message and your email address so we can reply and keep a record of the conversation. The free-audit popup on the Contact page opens a draft in your own email app. We receive that information only if you send the email.
  • Meetings. The Contact page embeds a Calendly scheduler (calendly.com/ozbarhama). If you book a time, Calendly collects the details its form asks for, usually your name and email, plus anything you type. We receive the booking. Calendly's own privacy policy is at calendly.com/privacy.

Information collected automatically

Every page loads Google Analytics 4, measurement ID G-H6T60VVJ27, from googletagmanager.com/gtag/js. It uses cookies (typically named _ga and a cookie starting with _ga_) and records online activity such as pages viewed, roughly how long a visit lasts, the site that referred you, browser and device type, and approximate location derived from your IP address. We use this to see which pages are used. It is an analytics tag, not an advertising pixel. Google's privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

Tools on specific pages

  • Patch designer. The pages at /pages/patch-design-ai-tool/ and /pages/patch-design-ai-tool-myka/ load a design tool from patchdesign.ai. If you use that tool, the design inputs you enter are handled by PatchDesign under that service's own practices. It is not an advertising pixel.
  • No advertising pixels. As of the date above, this site does not load a Meta (Facebook) Pixel, TikTok Pixel, Google Ads tag, LinkedIn Insight Tag, Pinterest tag, Hotjar, Microsoft Clarity, or a similar advertising pixel.

Cookies, analytics, and pixels

Cookies are small text files a site stores on your browser. This site uses them for Google Analytics, as described above. We do not use advertising cookies or pixel tags to follow you across other companies' websites, and we do not drop a tracking pixel in our own pages for ad networks.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. You can also install Google's Analytics opt-out add-on, described at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout. Blocking cookies does not stop the contact form or email from working.

How we use personal information

  • To answer contact-form messages and emails, and to schedule meetings you request.
  • To provide the products and services you ask about, including order and support messages when you email us.
  • To understand how the site is used, using Google Analytics, and to fix and improve it.
  • To keep ordinary business records and to meet legal duties, such as responding to a lawful request.
  • To protect the site, our customers, and Kinor LLC from fraud, abuse, and security incidents.

We do not sell mailing lists. We do not use your information for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not use profiling or automated decision-making that produces legal effects or similarly significant effects, such as decisions about credit, housing, employment, or insurance.

How we share personal information

We share personal information with service providers that process it for us:

  • FormSubmit receives contact-form submissions so it can email them to us.
  • Google receives analytics data through Google Analytics 4.
  • Calendly receives scheduling details if you book a meeting on the Contact page.
  • PatchDesign receives design inputs if you use the patch designer pages.

We may also share information if the law requires it, if we need to protect someone's rights or safety, or as part of a merger, sale, or similar business transfer. If that happens, the recipient will be asked to honor this policy for the information it receives.

We do not sell personal information for money. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising (sometimes called "targeted advertising" or "sharing" under US state privacy laws).

How long we keep it

We keep contact-form messages and emails for as long as we need them to reply, to keep ordinary business records, and to meet legal duties. After that we delete them or strip the details that identify you. Google keeps Analytics data according to the retention setting on our Analytics property. Calendly keeps booking records under its own settings.

Your choices, including Global Privacy Control

  • Email. You can ask us to stop emailing you by writing to info@kinorpartners.com. We will still send messages we need to send about a request you made, such as a reply to a support question.
  • Analytics cookies. Use your browser settings or the Google opt-out add-on linked above.
  • Do not sell or share. We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for targeted advertising. You can still email us a do-not-sell or do-not-share request. Use the subject line "Privacy request".
  • Global Privacy Control (GPC). GPC is a browser signal that tells websites not to sell or share your personal information. Because this site does not sell or share personal information, there is no sale or share for that signal to turn off. If your browser sends GPC and you want us to record an opt-out anyway, email info@kinorpartners.com with the subject "Privacy request" and say that your browser has GPC on. More about the signal is at globalprivacycontrol.org.

The same choices are summarized on our Privacy Choices page.

US state privacy rights

If you live in California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Texas, Oregon, or another US state with a consumer privacy law, you may have some or all of the rights below. California's law is the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA). Other states use similar rules. These rights are not the same in every state, and some requests can be refused where the law allows it.

  • Know and access. Ask what personal information we collected about you, the sources, the purposes, and the types of third parties we disclosed it to, and ask for a copy.
  • Correct. Ask us to fix inaccurate personal information.
  • Delete. Ask us to delete personal information we collected from you.
  • Opt out. Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, and opt out of targeted advertising. We do not do those things. You may still send the request.
  • Limit sensitive information. Ask us to limit the use of sensitive personal information. We do not ask for Social Security numbers, government ID, precise location, or account logins. We do not use personal information to infer characteristics about you. If you type extra details into a free-text message, we use that message only to respond.
  • Appeal. If we deny a request and your state's law gives you an appeal, email us and we will review it.
  • Non-discrimination. We will not deny service, charge a different price, or provide a different level of quality because you exercised these rights.

We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of anyone under 16.

How to send a request

Email info@kinorpartners.com with the subject "Privacy request". Tell us which right you want to use, the state you live in, and an email or phone number where we can reach you. We will respond within the time your state's law requires. That period is often 45 days, and we will tell you if we need more time where the law allows it.

We may need to confirm that the request is really from you. We will ask for details we already have, such as the email address you used with us. An authorized agent may submit a request for you. We may ask for signed permission from you, or a power of attorney where the law provides for one, and we may ask you to confirm that the agent may act for you.

If we deny your request, you can appeal by emailing the same address with the subject "Privacy appeal". Tell us the date of the denial and why you want it reviewed.
