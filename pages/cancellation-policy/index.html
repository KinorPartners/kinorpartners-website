Cancellation Policy

Last updated: June 4, 2026

This cancellation policy applies to recurring subscription plans offered by Kinor Partners, for example the Pro and Enterprise plans.

Billing and renewal

Subscription plans are billed on a recurring monthly basis and renew automatically at the end of each billing cycle until cancelled.

How to cancel

You may cancel your subscription at any time by sending written notice to oz@kinorpartners.com or through our Contact page. Please submit your cancellation request before your next renewal date.

When cancellation takes effect

When you cancel, your subscription remains active through the end of the current paid billing cycle and you will not be charged for any subsequent cycle. We do not provide refunds or credits for partial billing periods.

Need to cancel or have a question?

Email us at oz@kinorpartners.com and we'll take care of it.
